# Scope of the MIT licence and third-party materials

The root MIT licence covers original project software and the new repository
documentation. It does **not** relicense third-party datasets, articles, fonts,
dependencies, or author-owned manuscript PDFs. Scientific result files are
generated by the project; any embedded measurements retain their original
source attribution and applicable upstream terms.

Original source and row-provenance ledgers remain in `research/step11/`.
The public download metadata for two optional raw assets are retained, while
the raw CADS CSV and an unaccepted candidate publisher supplement are fetched
separately with `python scripts/fetch_optional_raw_assets.py`.
Those raw assets are not needed to check the bundled predictions or run the
documented frozen-input smoke check. Downloading a file does not grant extra
redistribution rights.

## Fonts and papers

- `paper/source/fonts/URW_Fonts_License.txt` retains the supplied URW/Nimbus
  font conditions. Do not apply MIT to these fonts.
- Droid Sans Fallback is an Android/Droid font. Its Apache 2.0 licence text is
  included as `paper/source/fonts/Droid_Apache-2.0.txt`.
- The latest English/Chinese manuscripts and supplementary materials retain
  their authors' copyright. No journal acceptance, published DOI, or public
  preregistration is claimed by this software release.
- The archived Nishimura article retains its CC BY 3.0 notice and attribution.

Public release preparation removed expired signed download query strings
without changing scientific arrays. Exact transformations and omissions are
listed in `docs/release_changes.json`; original archive checksums are retained
as historical provenance rather than silently rewritten.
