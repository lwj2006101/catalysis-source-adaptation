\section{S1 Dataset and provenance details}
\textbf{Methanol conversion/selectivity corpus (1,245 paired records, 82 sources, 80
leakage groups).} Rebuilt row-by-row from TheMeCat v1 (\texttt{TheMeCat\_v1.csv},
Zenodo record 17085762 v1.0, CC BY 4.0, 1,124 rows, SHA-256 46000f00\ldots) and the
Communications Engineering methanol synthesis file (\texttt{commseng\_datameoh.csv},
500 rows, SHA-256 0a81730a\ldots). Inclusion: both targets reported within $[0,100]$;
temperature, pressure, H$_2$/CO$_2$ positive and reported; GHSV/BET allowed missing
(imputed within training folds); no performance-based filtering; genuine zeros retained;
full-field duplicates kept once. Exclusions (row-level log): $-319$ no paired target,
$-26$ one target missing, $-30$ unresolved attribution/invalid DOI, $-1$ duplicate.
Twenty records whose BET had been reconstructed as $10^{\mathrm{LN}}$ were recomputed
from the stored natural-log field as $\exp(\mathrm{LN})$, logged.

\textbf{STY corpus (1,230 records, 109 sources).} Derived from the Suvarna et al.\
public dataset (CC BY 4.0); 20 DOIs overlap the conversion corpus (declared; treated as
a same-reaction different-target task). Four rows dropped for invalid target or missing
source mapping (logged).

\textbf{OCM corpus (1,794 records, 408 sources, 405 leakage groups).} From the Schmack
et al.\ meta-analysis supplement (CC BY 4.0): 1,802 raw catalyst records over 412
publication numbers; entries with missing/ambiguous references removed with a row-level
log; three pairs of identical-citation sources merged. The published corrected dataset
is used together with the original paper's processing description; it is not claimed to
be a raw experimental census.

\section{S2 Complete feature definitions}
\textbf{Methanol/STY operating features:} temperature (K), total pressure (bar),
H$_2$/CO$_2$ ratio (---), GHSV (L\,g$^{-1}$h$^{-1}$; STY corpus: cm$^3$\,h$^{-1}$g$^{-1}$
as published), BET surface area (m$^2$g$^{-1}$; methanol corpus only), catalyst amount
(STY corpus). \textbf{OCM F0:} temperature (K), CH$_4$ partial pressure (bar), O$_2$
partial pressure (bar), CH$_4$/O$_2$ ratio (---), total pressure (bar), contact time (s).
\textbf{OCM F1 adds:} up to four cation slots and two support slots with species name,
``listed'' indicator, known mol-fraction sum (mol\%$/100$), missing-amount count, and an
explicit unseen-species channel. Feature vocabulary (species names) is fitted on the
current training partition only; sodium is never treated as a missing marker; unlisted
species are ``unreported'', not proven absent. Response variables and their derivatives
are excluded from all feature sets.

\section{S3 Model hyperparameters}
\textbf{Base-model grid (inner source-grouped 3-fold, inner equal-weight MAE):} ridge
$\alpha\in\{0.1,1,10,100\}$; gradient-boosted trees $n\in\{100,300\}\times$depth
$\in\{2,3\}$ (learning rate 0.05, subsample 0.8, min-samples-split 3, seed 42) plus
$n=500$, depth 8. \textbf{Calibration hyper-parameters} (penalty $\lambda$ for affine,
$\alpha$ for local ridge, temperature ridge): selected on training-source pseudo-tasks
(ten pseudo-repeats per training source, five labels each). \textbf{Case-study models:}
author configurations reproduced as published (Suvarna: XGB colsample 0.5, eta 0.15,
depth 4, $n$=1000, subsample 1, seed 42; RF depth 40, sqrt, min-split 2, $n$=500; GBT
lr 0.05, depth 4, $n$=300, subsample 0.5. Xu: Optuna 100-trial tuning with record-level
cv=5, author's search spaces).

\section{S4 All-$k$ learning curves}
Source-equal MAE at $k=1,2,3,5,10$ (main cohort, random selector) for the eight display
methods on all four targets, with 95\% leakage-group bootstrap intervals, are provided in
the machine-readable registry (\texttt{FINAL\_NUMBER\_REGISTRY.csv},
\texttt{MASTER\_RESULTS.json}) and summarized in the main-text Table 3 ($k=5$) and
Table 4 ($k=1,5,10$ paired contrasts). The stacked $R^2$ is the predeclared primary
caliber; per-row-mean and per-seed calibers are archived alongside.

\section{S5 Low-budget small-source panel}
Sources whose calibration pool holds 3--9 records (methanol 22, OCM 45, STY 43) are
reported only at $k\le3$ and never mixed into main-cohort numbers. At $k=3$ the mean
offset reduces base MAE on this panel, mirroring the main-cohort picture; the corrected
$k=3$-only panel supersedes the earlier version that mixed pools.

\section{S6 Full paired contrasts}
Per-source paired contrasts (mean MAE difference, improved/harmed counts, bootstrap CIs)
for all method pairs at $k=1,5,10$ and all four targets are in
\texttt{MASTER\_RESULTS.json} (block \texttt{paired\_comparisons}) and the case-study
result JSONs. At $k=1$ the affine map equals the mean offset by construction and the
local ridge degenerates to a one-label constant.

\section{S7 Permutation and alignment details}
Twenty uniform random permutations per $A_k$ (identity allowed), shared between the
affine and local-ridge diagnostics; losses are computed per permutation and then
averaged. Real arms use calibration repeats 0--19 (identical to the permuted arms) and
reproduce the main-protocol cells within $6\times10^{-14}$. The diagnostic is
retrospective and is not a formal permutation significance test.

\section{S8 Allocation sensitivity}
Five frozen test-set allocations (0 = the fixed design used in all main tables; 1--4 =
independent hash-seeded allocations). Main-cohort MAE moves by $\le0.4$ pp (OCM) and
$\approx8$~mg\,h$^{-1}$g$_{cat}^{-1}$ (STY) at $k=5$; allocation 0 lies on the
favourable side for OCM-F1 local ridge (reported explicitly). Method rankings are
two-sided stable but not universally invariant (OCM-F1 local ridge: ranks 1--4).

\section{S9 Boundary sensitivity and negative predictions}
Primary predictions are never clipped. Bound sensitivity uses target-correct bounds:
percent targets $[0,100]$, STY $[0,\infty)$. The previous revision's universal
$[0,100]$ clip was an error (49.35\% of STY labels exceed 100): it inflated the STY
local-ridge $k{=}10$ MAE to 109.1 instead of 54.5. Negative base predictions were absent
in all sampled parts.

\section{S10 Suvarna 2022 reproduction details}
Repository commit \texttt{0dac9383} (MIT). The notebook loads \texttt{Combined
New\_V1.xlsx}, which is not public; four documented compatibility fixes were applied to
the published 1,234-row dataset file. Author-committed outputs (XGB test $R^2$ 0.9132,
RMSE 77.27) are the reference. Our close reproduction with per-fold train-only
preprocessing: XGB 0.9210 ($+0.008$), RF 0.8806 ($-0.007$), GBT 0.9138 ($+0.012$).
The controlled record-vs-source contrast (same rows, features, configs; only the split
differs): XGB source-equal MAE 57.9$\to$124.0, stacked $R^2$ 0.876$\to$0.319; nested 130.3--135.3. The strict F17-vs-F5 paired comparison (same rows/folds; F5 contains no
categorical columns): record-level splits favour F17 for all three models; under
source-disjoint evaluation GBT/RF advantages vanish and only XGB retains a significant
advantage ($-29.6$ [$-42.7,-17.1$]). The earlier ``F5'' that contained four categorical
columns is renamed F9 (reduced set) and is SI-only; the cross-dataset ``buy back
$\approx4$ units'' comparison is withdrawn.

\section{S12 Preprocessing leakage tests}
Per-fold fitting is verified by an automated test: with held-out rows tampered
(categories replaced by unseen strings, numerics set to extreme values), refitting the
preprocessing on the same training rows yields bit-identical category maps and medians;
unseen test categories encode to $-1$ and do not extend the vocabulary. The test is part
of the integration suite (sixteen tests) and runs in the clean-run pipeline.

\section{S13 Source registries and provenance summary}
Per-corpus registries (\texttt{canonical\_source\_id}, \texttt{leakage\_group\_id},
per-row upstream file/row references) and exclusion logs ship with the reproduction
package; case-study registries (\texttt{XU\_SOURCE\_REGISTRY.csv},
\texttt{XU\_ROW\_PROVENANCE.csv}, \texttt{SUVARNA\_*}) record the raw-to-canonical DOI
normalization (157 raw strings $\to$ 156 canonical, 1 merge) and the 82 DOI-missing rows
(excluded from the main audit, sensitivity only).

\section{S14 Reproducibility environment}
Python 3.13.15; numpy 2.5.2; pandas 3.0.5; scikit-learn 1.9.0; xgboost 3.4.1;
case-study additions: catboost 1.2.10, lightgbm, optuna 5.0.0, shap, seaborn. BLAS
threads pinned to one. Cluster bootstrap: 100{,}000 draws, seed base 510{,}927,
chunked. Original-author environments differ (not bit-identical); reproduction classes
reflect this. All main-script logs end with exit code 0; failed earlier attempts are
archived under \texttt{history/failed\_runs} and marked FAILED.
