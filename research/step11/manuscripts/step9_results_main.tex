\subsection{Acquisition chemistry and prediction chemistry have distinct roles}
The executed OCM follow-up strengthens the representation crossing while adding two design controls (Table~\ref{tab:step9matched}; Fig.~\ref{fig:step9geometry}). On the twenty-two historical eligible sources, composition V changes the frozen F0 blend's MAE from 3.705 under random supports to 3.370, even though F0 does not consume catalyst composition. With F1, the corresponding errors are 3.092 and 2.708. These retrospective results locate information useful for acquisition separately from information used by prediction. Shuffling the chemistry row correspondence for acquisition, while retaining the actual predictor inputs, yields MAEs of 3.647 and 3.039 for F0 and F1, respectively. The comparison is a selector mechanism diagnostic, not a causal claim about an element's catalytic action.

The learned conservative gate does not improve the fixed composition design. Its locked historical primary MAE is 3.373, compared with 3.370 for composition V, 3.821 for operating V, 3.458 for kernel V and 3.379 for robust multi-view V. None of its five adjusted contrasts supports superiority (the SI primary-contrast table). It retains composition acquisition in 109 of 110 primary source/seed episodes; this 99.1\% fallback rate explains its near-equality with the fixed rule. The gate without the empirical margin has MAE 3.569. Additional tuning and a context regressor are therefore not, by themselves, a validated algorithmic contribution. The positive evidence resides in the controlled separation of acquisition and response information and its evaluated deployment scope.

\subsection{New measured-source data support calibration and informative locations}
The frozen published-source application retains a same-budget acquisition benefit. For F1, composition V reduces the blend MAE from 4.787 under fifty random support draws to 4.213 percentage points, a 12.0\% point improvement. The operating V result is 4.483. For F0, composition V improves on random acquisition from 5.476 to 5.039, but operating V performs better at 4.817. Thus the historical ordering of acquisition views for F0 does not persist on this source. The learned gate selects composition and matches its loss; it neither resolves this reversal nor establishes an advantage over the fixed controls. These are single-family observations, without a cross-source confidence claim.

The new data also retain the distinction between response level and input--response correspondence (Fig.~\ref{fig:step9predictors}). On the same composition-V supports and F1 query, the unadapted historical model has MAE 5.869, a five-response support mean has 5.959, a support-local linear fit has 4.170, and frozen blending has 4.213. A local RBF with historical-only policy tuning has 3.813. The extra historical response structure does not outperform this local kernel control. With only five labels, a feature-dependent source-local fit can recover useful response structure that a source level alone misses; these observations support conditional calibration, rather than improved arbitrary-source zero-label generalization. Missing composition amounts and four operating variables delimit this particular application, and the selected discovery catalysts do not represent an unbiased population of all OCM materials.

\subsection{Execution and independent checks}
All twenty-five historical comparisons were executed in the authorized current runtime, producing 93150 evaluation cells and 7948800 scalar query predictions. Independent loss reaggregation checks every scalar and reconstructs all 9315 support orders; prediction reconstruction checks 105600 query-row records, using the released prediction helpers. Separate numerical checks compare posterior variance algebra and ridge/leave-one-out results with direct references. Poisoning the actual target outcome columns leaves all eligible source partitions unchanged. The final pipeline contains fifty-six newly fitted backbone models. The new-cohort application has 1380 evaluation cells and 7443720 scalar predictions; every scalar loss and all 138 support orders are audited, with 98890 query-row predictions reconstructed. These are finite computations and implementation checks, not a second independent wet-laboratory replication.
