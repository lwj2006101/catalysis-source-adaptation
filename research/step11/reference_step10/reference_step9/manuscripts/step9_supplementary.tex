\section{Executed acquisition-view follow-up}
\subsection{Scientific scope and finite choices}
This follow-up contains eleven acquisition policies and twelve predictors for each OCM prediction representation. The policies are random; operating V; composition V; feature coverage; pool V; kernel V; robust multi-view V; fixed historical best view; a zero-margin view gate; the empirical-margin conservative gate; and composition-correspondence-shuffled V. Nested budgets are 1, 2, 3, 5 and 10. Random acquisition has fifty target draws and ten historical tuning draws; chemistry shuffling has ten draws. The same support order is crossed with both F0 and F1. Gate development targets F0's frozen blend at five labels, and therefore does not claim to optimize every other predictor or budget.

The original nine response methods are joined by policy-retuned local ridge, blend and local RBF. Ridge penalties are $\{0.1,1,10,100\}$. The local RBF uses the inherited nine $(\alpha,\gamma)$ combinations from $\alpha\in\{0.01,0.1,1\}$ and $\gamma\in\{0.1,1,10\}$. Blending uses the inherited finite prior-weight and penalty grid. Scores are averaged over draws and equally over historical pseudo-sources. The acquisition kernel's gamma is selected using historical F0 blend losses. The robust design is a finite greedy maximum-remaining-variance-fraction control; it is not globally optimized over all support subsets.

The operating kernel is $K_O(x,x')=100+x_O^Tx'_O$, and composition V uses the corresponding full-feature linear kernel. Kernel V uses $K_C(x,x')=100+\exp(-\gamma\|x_C-x'_C\|^2/d_C)$. For a support set $A$, the mean query working variance is
\[
 V(A)=\overline{K(q,q)}-\frac{1}{n_Q}\mathrm{tr}\{K_{QA}(K_{AA}+\alpha I)^{-1}K_{AQ}\}.
\]
Robust selection minimizes $\max\{V_O(A)/V_O(\varnothing),V_C(A)/V_C(\varnothing)\}$ at each greedy step. The alpha schedule is inherited from F1 and shared by the two views. These posterior calculations are working design criteria, without a calibrated-risk assertion.

\subsection{Empirical view gate and a bounded theoretical interpretation}
The gate fits eight fixed response-free contexts: log pool size, log query size, log operating variation, log chemistry variation, log query chemistry novelty relative to the pool, chemistry variation unexplained by a pool-only ridge on operating features, log chemistry mean shift, and the Jaccard disagreement between the two five-point support sets. The ridge penalty is fixed at 10. Fewer than six historical groups trigger composition fallback. Otherwise group-held-out mean absolute meta errors supply the 80th-percentile margin $q$. Meta fitting assigns inverse within-group frequency weights. Original adapter settings had been selected on the entire outer-training set, so this margin is not fully nested calibration of every dependency.

Write $\delta=L_C-L_O$, with estimated difference $\widehat\delta$. A zero-margin sign choice has pointwise regret at most $|\widehat\delta-\delta|$ relative to the better of the two realized losses. For the empirical-margin rule $O$ iff $\widehat\delta>q$, with $q\geq0$, regret is at most $|\widehat\delta-\delta|+q$; the excess loss from an incorrect switch relative to staying with $C$ is at most $\max\{|\widehat\delta-\delta|-q,0\}$. These elementary identities follow directly by considering the two signs of $\delta$. They do not control the meta prediction error under source shift, provide a coverage guarantee, or constitute a novel learning theorem. Transductive experimental design and kernel integrated-variance selection have substantial prior literature\cite{yubi,gorodetsky2016}; the current evaluated contribution is the catalysis representation crossing and measured information decomposition.

\section{New measurement curation and frozen application}
\subsection{Inclusion, units and original experiment identity}
The author dataset of Nishimura et al.\ \cite{nishimura2023new} reports measured temperature curves, validation phases, furnace length and feed ratio. Its 2928 rows include water-summary lines, repeated conditions and controls. We include only explicitly identified SVR-1, SVR-2 and Bayesian-1/2/3 validation phases, whose original study describes new catalyst preparation and testing. Mixed literature/HTS List0, proposed/predicted lists, \texttt{Unpublished}, \texttt{STD}, and \texttt{Refernce/reference} blocks are excluded. The retained population contains 1739 rows, 116 unique metal/support catalyst identities, one publication and one experiment family. The exclusion ledger records 1024 phase exclusions, 120 incomplete temperature/response entries and 45 repeated catalyst conditions. The first CSV occurrence per furnace/feed/slot-invariant composition/support/temperature signature is retained. No query responses are averaged into a support response.

Decimal commas become decimal points and Celsius becomes Kelvin by adding 273.15. Only explicitly reported feed ratios are used. The author protocol reports a 270 mm furnace, 21/7/3 mL min$^{-1}$ CH$_4$/O$_2$/N$_2$ and 50 mg catalyst, but these facts do not supply an unambiguous gas residence volume or explicitly resolved pressure; total pressure, reactant partial pressures and contact time remain missing. The missing fractions for all four fields are 1.0. Historical-only medians are used by the frozen transformer. Precursor mmol per support mass is not converted into the historical mol-percent basis. All mol-percent fields remain missing. Single-cation oxide supports map to their support-cation symbols while original formulas are retained; oxidation state or morphology is not inferred.

Reported ethane and ethylene carbon yields sum to C$_2$ yield within 0.1 percentage points, and methane conversion minus reported carbon products agrees with missing carbon within 0.2 percentage points, consistent with one-decimal rounding and the original N$_2$ internal-standard procedure. The carbon-basis endpoint is not interpreted as an outlet mole fraction. Labels outside physical bounds would be excluded with a reason, never clipped; no such exclusion was required among eligible complete responses.

Rounded fingerprint screening finds nine old-row matches for three low-yield Na--Mn--W/SiO$_2$ records: 0.0\% at 600/650 $^{\circ}$C and 0.5\% at 700 $^{\circ}$C, versus old 2011 records of 0.0/0.6\%. The new paper explicitly includes that catalyst among its newly prepared SVR-1 validation measurements. These nonspecific low-yield coincidences do not establish copied experimental runs; the full audit and review notes are retained. Original measurement origin, phase tracing and exclusion of mixed/reused inputs support new publication-level application. They do not certify all historical laboratory identities or full catalyst-batch independence. All phases are therefore one inference group.

\subsection{Chronology, freezing and implementation verification}
The twenty-five historical source/fold comparisons were completed and audited before full-history fitting. The final pipeline fits 54 group-OOF backbone candidates and two full backbones, selects controls using historical responses only, and freezes 79 code/model/parameter/input hashes before raw candidate download and new-cohort scoring. Public paper conclusions and the CADS landing page had informed screening before this stage; the source is not a prospectively collected campaign or public preregistered test. The included row outcomes were not used to select the gate or to retune the frozen controls. Curation is locked before the first numerical application.

Historical execution exports 93150 cells, 662400 query-row records and 7948800 scalar predictions. All scalar losses are independently reaggregated, all 9315 support orders reconstructed, and 105600 query-row predictions regenerated through the prediction helpers. Actual outcome poisoning tests 2040 source/split frames, including 110 eligible frames, comparing 270 eligible partition index arrays. Separate numerical checks validate dual/primal variance equality, ridge query and exact leave-one-out values against direct scikit-learn fits, and equivalent-view robust/linear order equality. The published-source application exports 1380 cells, 620310 query-row records and 7443720 scalar values; all scalar losses and 138 support orders pass auditing and 98890 query-row predictions are reconstructed. The reconstruction reuses prediction helpers; only the loss reaggregation and direct math references are independent implementations. None of these checks is a new biological/chemical replication or a pipeline-refit bootstrap.

The ACS 2024 SI candidate was downloaded and its publisher-provided MD5 verified. Its experimental Figure S8 provides plotted replicates for three catalysts, without a complete compatible numeric row table in that asset; it is not silently digitized and counted as a second new dataset. The additional methanol candidate remains unevaluated because its publisher landing access returns HTTP403 and no compatible SI rows were acquired. These sources contribute no extra source count or numerical performance claim.

\subsection{Portable reproduction and opened-cohort boundary}
The new root \texttt{protocol/runtime\_authorization.json} records the user's explicit replacement of the earlier private-server-only constraint. Entry points permit the authorized current runtime and keep outputs inside the project. \texttt{server/run\_history.sh} performs the math check, real one-part smoke, full historical grid, audit, aggregation and final freezing. Archived Step7 bases and OOF responses are inputs to the historical extension, not claimed new outer refits. The original prepared Step8 guard/instructions are retained as predecessor provenance.

\texttt{analysis/curate\_nishimura.py} writes all inclusion, provenance, missing-unit and overlap files; \texttt{code/evaluate\_external\_ocm.py} verifies the freeze hashes and applies the same fixed pipeline once. The manuscript sources and \texttt{analysis/build\_step9\_results.py} regenerate the measured figures/tables without fitting. The completed new cohort is now opened evidence. Any further gate redesign must treat it as development and obtain a different untouched publication/laboratory or a prospectively locked experiment for confirmation.
